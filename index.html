Questions

Do I need experience?

Not at all. Every class has beginner options and coaches adjust moves for you.

Can I freeze my membership?

Yes, up to 60 days per year at no cost.

Is there a contract?

No. All plans are month to month, or yearly if you want the discount.

What should I bring?

Water, a towel and comfortable shoes. We provide mats and equipment.

Do you offer personal training?

PT sessions are included in Elite and available to add on for any plan.
